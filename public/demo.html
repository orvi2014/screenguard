ScreenGuard demo

A safe sandbox that behaves like a page of search results. Nothing here is a real website.

  1. Hover a flagged result — a warning appears next to the link.
  2. Click one — the navigation is stopped before it starts.
  3. Choose Open anyway — ScreenGuard steps aside and the browser tries the address.
  4. Turn on “I’m sharing my screen” in the popup, then reload — the values at the bottom blur.

The flagged links point at reserved .test addresses that intentionally do not resolve, so “Open anyway” ends at a browser “site can’t be reached” page. That is the demo working.
